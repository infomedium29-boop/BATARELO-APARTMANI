The entire villa is yours

Villa Batarelo sits in the quiet village of Lučane near Sinj. With three bedrooms, a living room, a kitchen and generous outdoor areas, it is a comfortable base for families and friends travelling together.

The private pool, dining terrace and garden make it easy to enjoy slow days at home. Your host Davor is available to answer questions before and during your stay.
